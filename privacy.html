Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 • Published by Thoughts To Pen Consulting Corporation

1. Core Philosophy: Ephemerality by Architecture

At Thoughts To Pen Consulting Corporation ("Thoughts to Pen", "we", "us", or "our"), privacy is not an afterthought or an opt-in toggle; it is the fundamental foundation of Finger Trail.

Traditional messaging platforms retain your texts, images, and drawings permanently on centralized cloud servers. Finger Trail was engineered from the ground up with a zero-server-storage architecture: we do not store your drawings, gestures, or messages on any server database.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

When you use the Finger Trail mobile application:

  • No Account Information: You do not need to provide a phone number, email address, password, or username.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, physical address, contacts, or phone book.
  • No Message Content: Your drawings, strokes, color choices, and replay timelines are never transmitted to our servers.
  • No Device Fingerprints or Advertising IDs: The native mobile app contains no advertising SDKs, IDFA/GAID trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • No Precise Location Data: We do not track or request your GPS or device location.

3. How Gesture Notes Work (Zero Server Footprint)

When you create a drawing in Finger Trail:

  1. Your gesture coordinates and velocity curves are compressed mathematically into a compact binary representation.
  2. This data is appended directly to the URL fragment identifier (the section following the # symbol). According to standard Internet RFC standards, URL fragments are processed purely on the user's client device and are never sent to the hosting web server in HTTP requests.
  3. When your recipient opens the link, their device reads the URL fragment directly into volatile device RAM, reconstructs the strokes, and plays them back.
  4. Once playback finishes, the 8.0-second self-destruct fuse expires, and the vector data is immediately and permanently purged from device memory.

4. Website Analytics

Our marketing landing page at https://fingertrail.thoughtstopen.com utilizes standard Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymous website traffic metrics (such as page views and general geographical country). This aggregated web data contains no personal identifiers and cannot be associated with any gesture drawing. The native mobile application does not include Google Analytics.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

Because we do not collect, store, or possess your personal data or drawings, we cannot and do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties under any circumstances.

6. Children's Privacy

Finger Trail is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
