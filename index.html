Core Architecture

Designed for emotional intimacy, not permanence

Modern messaging traps every fleeting emotion in permanent, searchable corporate databases. Finger Trail brings back the intimacy of whispers and temporary notes.

01

Living Stroke Replay

Recipients don't just see a static drawing; they watch your gestures recreate your handwriting in real time with authentic velocity, pauses, and pressure.

02

8-Second Self-Destruct

The moment the replay reaches its final stroke, a high-tension 8.0-second countdown activates. At 0.0s, the canvas bursts into fading embers and is purged from device memory.

03

Zero Server Footprint

Notes are encoded into cryptographically compressed URL hashes. No central message database exists. If the link is closed, the message cannot be recovered.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can recipients take screenshots?

On native mobile apps, Finger Trail employs operating-system-level secure window flags (FLAG_SECURE on Android) that prevent screenshots and screen recordings. On web, the 8-second timer and motion replay ensure minimal exposure.

Do recipients need to install the app?

No. If the recipient does not have the native mobile app installed, the link opens seamlessly in any modern web browser on fingertrail.thoughtstopen.com, playing back the note instantly without installing anything.

Where are my drawings stored?

Nowhere. The entire gesture vector path is compressed mathematically into the link fragment (after the #). It is never sent to our servers, never saved to disk, and vanishes the moment the session ends.

Who created Finger Trail?

Finger Trail is developed and published by Thoughts to Pen, founded by Aman Rana, dedicated to building intimate, focused, and respectful creative software.
